Visual System

Direction

Dark, exact, one accent.

The website uses a dark dot grid, a rotary dial, and a dot-matrix readout. It shows one section at a time.

Use one rose accent for the active state only. Avoid gradients, extra colors, stock imagery, and unnecessary effects. Documents and PDFs stay black and white.

Color

Background

#080808

Text

#edebe6

Accent

#f07383

Muted

#7b7a77

Website dark theme. Documents use black #000000, white #ffffff, and gray only.

Typography

Website

Use Geist for text and Geist Mono for labels and numbers. Use sentence case and short headings.

Documents

Use a condensed bold sans for titles and a monospace for body text and numbers.

White Rose Brand guide / 02
Logo Usage

Logo

Use the logo files in site/assets. Keep the mark square and preserve equal padding on all sides.

Rules

  • Use the SVG marks on the website and the PNG tiles in documents.
  • Use the logo only in black and white. Do not use the accent color on the logo.
  • Do not stretch, skew, rotate, recolor, or crop the logo tighter.
  • Do not add shadows, glows, outlines, or effects.
  • Do not place the logo over photography or patterns.
  • Keep at least one quarter of the logo width as clear space around the mark.

Working Model

Working Model

  • Understand the system.
  • Explain the tradeoffs.
  • Help decide what to build, simplify, or avoid.
  • Support implementation when needed.
  • Keep recommendations clear and practical.

Name

Use White Rose.

Use `White Rose` as the primary written name. `Whiterose` can be used as a secondary styling when needed.

The name should be tied to clear software engineering work, not floral or decorative language.
